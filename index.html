WHO WE ARE

Conversations that go beyond the obvious.

Flickable India brings together real people, real experiences, and real conversations — giving stories the space they truly deserve.

We don't just talk about what happened. We explore the journey behind it — the ideas, struggles, decisions, failures, and achievements that make every story unique.

From public figures and entrepreneurs to doctors, artists, creators, professionals, and inspiring individuals, we bring diverse voices to one platform.

OUR VISION

Building conversations that go beyond the surface.

To build a platform where meaningful conversations bring forward diverse voices, real journeys, and perspectives that shape India.

We envision Flickable India as a trusted digital platform where every conversation has a purpose, every story has value, and every voice has the opportunity to inspire, inform, and create impact.

OUR MISSION

Honest conversations. Authentic storytelling.

Our mission is to create honest, engaging, and meaningful conversations with people from different walks of life.

Through thoughtful conversations and authentic storytelling, Flickable India aims to inform, inspire, connect, and give audiences a deeper perspective on the people and stories that matter.

UPCOMING GUEST
UPCOMING CONVERSATION

RAHILA FIRDOUS

Rahila Firdous is a Bhopal-born cricketer who has carved her own path in women's cricket as a right-handed wicketkeeper-batter and leader.

From representing Madhya Pradesh to stepping onto the Women’s Premier League stage, Rahila’s journey is a story of ambition, leadership and persistence.

A product of Bhopal, Rahila has represented Madhya Pradesh Women, India C Women, Central Zone Women and Bundelkhand Bulls Women. Her leadership and performances in domestic cricket earned her a place with Mumbai Indians Women in the WPL 2026, marking another important chapter in her cricketing journey.
